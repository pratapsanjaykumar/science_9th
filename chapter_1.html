What is Science?

"Science is not merely memorizing conclusions—it is the process of testing, questioning, measuring, and uncovering the truth of nature."

✔ Middle Stage Science
Science - 9th

In earlier classes, science focused on:

observing nature
asking simple questions
basic experiments

At that stage, the goal was to develop curiosity.

Example:
You learned that plants need sunlight, but you did not study how this process works in detail.

  • • In secondary stage, science becomes deep exploration.

  • ✔ • reasoning

Science - 9th

Science is not only about "what we know", but also about:

• how we know it
✔
• how we test it
✔
• how we improve it
✔

Science is not only about: "What do we know?"

Science is also about: "How do we know it?"

This is one of the most important ideas in all science.

This makes scientific knowledge reliable and trustworthy.

Process of Scientific Thinking
Science - 9th

Suppose someone says:

"Plants grow better in sunlight."

A scientist will ask:

  • ✔ How do we know this?
  • • Was it tested? ✔
  • • What evidence supports it? ✔
  • • Were experiments performed? ✔
  • • Can others repeat the experiment?

This careful thinking is scientific thinking.

"Plants grow better in sunlight" — Evidence & Testing

Observation
Science - 9th
  • • Observation means carefully noticing things using our senses or instruments.
  • • It is the starting point of all scientific investigations. ✔
  • • Good observation requires attention and accuracy.
Example:
Noticing that clouds become dark before it rains.
✔ Measurement
Science - 9th
  • • Measurement means expressing observations in numbers using standard units.
  • • It makes observations more accurate and reliable.
  • • Without measurement, scientific study would be vague.
Example:
Instead of saying "it is hot", we say "temperature is 35°C".
